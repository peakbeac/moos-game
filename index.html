Coral County

A drivable coast split into beach, desert, city, and mountains. Traffic lights change, trains take the loop, and the runway faces east.

  • Spawn is on Shell Beach, beside the blue Lumen iX.
  • Palm Circuit, Mesa Oval, and Ridge Loop are marked in red on the map.
  • Tide Line stops at the stations. The yellow Rail Bug is yours.

WASD to move · F to drive, fly, or board · M for the map

County map

Click the map to drop a beacon. Roads are white, rails are pale, tracks are coral.

How to get around

On foot
WASD or arrows, mouse drag to look, Space to jump.
Cars
W throttle, S brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Shift boost, L headlights, H horn.
Planes
W/S throttle, A/D roll, arrow up nose up, arrow down nose down. Gull Trainer is lined up on runway 09.
Helicopters
W/S lean, A/D turn, Space climb, C descend. They hover when you let go.
Trains
F beside a stopped Tide Line coach to ride. F on the yellow Rail Bug to drive the inner loop.
Other
R resets your current ride. M opens the map. N mutes.
